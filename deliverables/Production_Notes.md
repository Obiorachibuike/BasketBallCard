## Scientific and visual context

Brain-computer interfaces already exist; uploading a complete human mind and transferring consciousness are speculative and have not been demonstrated. Artificial neural networks are simplified computational models, not complete copies of biological brains.

The laboratory and futuristic images are AI-generated conceptual illustrations, not footage of actual participants or experiments. The video combines animated camera moves over this artwork with original motion graphics; it is not fully character-animated 3D footage. Neural traces are illustrative, not recorded patient data.
